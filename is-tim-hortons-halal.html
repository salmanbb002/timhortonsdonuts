Is Tim Hortons Halal in Canada?

What chain-wide certification means, how meat is sourced, and which menu items are pork- and alcohol-free.

← Back to the full menu

Short Answer

No. Tim Hortons does not hold chain-wide halal certification in Canada. Chicken, beef and other meat products used across Canadian locations are not sourced from halal-certified suppliers for the mainstream menu.

A Few Locations Are the Exception

A small number of individually owned locations, mostly in Ontario, have obtained their own local halal certification. This applies only to that specific restaurant, not to Tim Hortons as a chain — so it's not something you can assume at a random location.

What About Non-Meat Items?

Coffee, tea, donuts, bagels and muffins generally don't contain pork or alcohol-based ingredients. That said, shared prep equipment and fryers mean cross-contamination with meat products (like bacon or sausage) is possible, so these items aren't formally halal-certified either.

How to Check a Specific Location

If halal certification matters for your order, the only reliable approach is to contact the specific restaurant directly and ask, since certification (where it exists at all) is set at the individual franchise level, not company-wide.
