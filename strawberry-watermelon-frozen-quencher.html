Strawberry Watermelon Frozen Quencher is the frozen version of Strawberry Watermelon in the Tim Hortons Fruit Quenchers lineup. It is listed in 3 sizes: Small $3.49, Medium $3.99, Large $4.49, with each size step adding $0.50.

Other ways to order Strawberry Watermelon

Strawberry Watermelon is also listed as a Lemonade Quencher (S $2.29 · M $2.79 · L $3.29) and a Sparkling Quencher (S $2.79 · M $3.29 · L $3.79). Across the sizes all three share, the Frozen version is the highest-priced of the three.

Common questions

How much is a medium Strawberry Watermelon Frozen Quencher?

$3.99 CAD before tax, as last verified. Prices vary by location and province.

Is it priced differently from other Frozen Quenchers?

No. Blackberry Yuzu, Orange Tangerine, Peach and Pineapple Dragon Fruit Frozen Quenchers are listed at the same prices; Wildberry Hibiscus is listed at M $3.89 · L $4.39.

Is the Strawberry Watermelon Frozen Quencher caffeine-free and dairy-free?

Independent menu guides consistently list Fruit Quenchers as caffeine-free and dairy-free. If you have an allergy, confirm with Tim Hortons' official allergen information before ordering.

About Fruit Quenchers

In a June 14, 2021 press release, Tim Hortons said its Real Fruit Quenchers “are made from real fruit juice from concentrate.”

That release launched Strawberry Watermelon alongside Peach. Hope Bagozzi, Tim Hortons’ Chief Marketing Officer, said the two new flavours are “part of our commitment to providing guests with menu items that are made without artificial colours or flavours.”
