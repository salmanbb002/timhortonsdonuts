About This Site

TimHortonsDonuts.com is an independent, fan-run reference for the Tim Hortons donut menu in Canada. We list prices and calories for every donut on the current menu, plus a short guide to the different donut styles, so you can compare options before you order. We also cover the rest of the menu and seasonal releases, from the coffee menu to current Tim Hortons Pumpkin Spice prices, the charity Smile Cookie and the Tim Hortons holiday menu. For background on the company, see who owns Tim Hortons, how many Tim Hortons there are in Canada and where to find Tim Hortons in the U.S. For our verdict on the food and coffee, read our honest Tim Hortons review, and for fan-made custom orders, see the Tim Hortons secret menu. To see how our prices compare with the competition, read our Tim Hortons vs Starbucks price comparison. For items that are no longer sold, see which Tim Hortons items have been discontinued, and for ordering ideas, read our pick of the best Tim Hortons drinks. We also compare Tims with Dunkin' and with McDonald's coffee. For what's on now, see the fall menu, all new 2026 menu items and summer drinks.

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Tim Hortons or Restaurant Brands International. Product names, trademarks and photographs remain the property of their respective owners.

Prices are reference estimates in Canadian dollars, before tax, and can vary by location and over time. Always check current pricing and availability with your local restaurant or the official Tim Hortons app before ordering.
