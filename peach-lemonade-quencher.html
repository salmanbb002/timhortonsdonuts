Peach Lemonade Quencher is the lemonade version of Peach in the Tim Hortons Fruit Quenchers lineup. It is listed in 3 sizes: Small $2.29, Medium $2.79, Large $3.29, with each size step adding $0.50.

Other ways to order Peach

Peach is also listed as a Sparkling Quencher (S $2.79 · M $3.29 · L $3.79) and a Frozen Quencher (S $3.49 · M $3.99 · L $4.49). Across the sizes all three share, the Lemonade version is the lowest-priced of the three.

Common questions

How much is a medium Peach Lemonade Quencher?

$2.79 CAD before tax, as last verified. Prices vary by location and province.

Is it priced differently from other Lemonade Quenchers?

No. Blackberry Yuzu, Orange Tangerine, Pineapple Dragon Fruit and Strawberry Watermelon Lemonade Quenchers are listed at the same prices; Wildberry Hibiscus is listed at S $2.19 · M $2.69.

Is the Peach Lemonade Quencher caffeine-free and dairy-free?

Independent menu guides consistently list Fruit Quenchers as caffeine-free and dairy-free. If you have an allergy, confirm with Tim Hortons' official allergen information before ordering.

About Fruit Quenchers

In a June 14, 2021 press release, Tim Hortons said its Real Fruit Quenchers “are made from real fruit juice from concentrate.”

That release launched Peach alongside Strawberry Watermelon. Hope Bagozzi, Tim Hortons’ Chief Marketing Officer, said the two new flavours are “part of our commitment to providing guests with menu items that are made without artificial colours or flavours.”
