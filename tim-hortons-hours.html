What Time Does Tim Hortons Open and Close?

Typical opening and closing times, breakfast cutoff, and how to find the exact hours for your local restaurant.

← Back to the full menu

Typical Opening & Closing Times

Hours vary by location, but most of the roughly 3,900 Tim Hortons restaurants in Canada open early and close late:

Weekdays~5–6 AM to 10 PM–12 AM
Saturday & Sunday~6–7 AM to 10 PM–12 AM
High-traffic / highway locationsSome open 24 hours (drive-thru)

Breakfast Hours

Tim Hortons has served breakfast all day at participating restaurants in Canada since July 25, 2018, so breakfast sandwiches and hash browns are usually available until closing. Some items are morning-only, though — the Scrambled Egg Loaded Wraps launched in 2025 as a 4 a.m. to 11 a.m. item — and franchise locations set their own hours. See the full breakfast menu for prices.

Holiday Hours

Hours may be reduced or locations may close entirely on statutory holidays such as Christmas Day and New Year's Day. Individual, franchise-owned locations set their own holiday schedules.

Find Your Exact Local Hours

Because hours differ by city, province and restaurant type, the most reliable way to confirm hours for a specific location is the official Tim Hortons app or website store locator, or by calling the restaurant directly.
