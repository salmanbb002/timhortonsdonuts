How We Source and Verify Our Prices

TimHortonsDonuts.com is an independent, fan-run reference. We're not affiliated with Tim Hortons or Restaurant Brands International, and we don't pull data from their internal systems.

Every price and calorie figure here starts from publicly available menu reference material, which we cross-check against the official Tim Hortons app each quarter.

Prices vary by province, location, and over time, so we periodically re-check listings against the official app and update pages when something changes. Each item page shows a "prices last verified" date so you know how current that page is.

Spotted a wrong price, a discontinued item, or a bad calorie count? Email us — we review every report and correct confirmed errors promptly.

For the final word on price and availability near you, the official Tim Hortons app is always the most accurate source.
