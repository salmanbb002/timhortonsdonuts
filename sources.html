Sources

Menu item names, listed prices and calorie figures on this site are reference data compiled from public Tim Hortons Canada menu reference sites. They are estimates in Canadian dollars, before tax, and are not pulled live from Tim Hortons systems.

Menu item photographs are used for identification purposes and remain the property of Tim Hortons / Restaurant Brands International or the original source site. No ownership of any product name, logo or photograph is claimed by this site.

Blog article photographs that are not Tim Hortons product shots are used under free licenses: the "Are Tim Hortons Donuts Baked or Fried?" featured photo is by Laslovarga via Wikimedia Commons, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

Prices, availability and calorie information can change at any time and can vary by location. Always confirm current details with the official Tim Hortons app or your local restaurant before ordering.
