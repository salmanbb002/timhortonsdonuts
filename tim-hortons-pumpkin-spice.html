Tim Hortons Pumpkin Spice 2026: The Full Lineup, Dates and Calories

Five Pumpkin Spice items are back in Canada until November 1 — what each one is, what it costs, and what a pump of syrup adds.

About the author: Salman B. — independently built and maintains TimHortonsDonuts.com, cross-checking prices and menu details against the official Tim Hortons app each quarter. More about the author

Full Menu / Blog / Pumpkin Spice

Tim Hortons Pumpkin Spice is back in Canada for fall 2026. The Pumpkin Spice Latte, Iced Latte, Iced Capp, Cold Brew and Muffin returned on September 1, 2026 and are available until November 1, 2026 at participating restaurants. Each pump of Pumpkin Spice syrup adds 25 calories and 5 g of sugar.

When Is Tim Hortons Pumpkin Spice Available in 2026?

Tim Hortons Canada launched its 2026 Pumpkin Spice lineup on September 1, 2026, and it runs until November 1, 2026. That gives you about nine weeks, and as of September 27 about five are left.

The dates differ across the border and from last year. Tim Hortons U.S. started its fall menu earlier, on August 12, 2026. In Canada, the 2025 lineup arrived in August, so this year's September 1 start was a later return.

"Participating restaurants" matters here. Tim Hortons restaurants are franchised, and a location can run out of syrup or muffins before the official end date. If you are ordering in late October, check the item in the Tim Hortons app first.

What's on the Tim Hortons Pumpkin Spice Menu?

The 2026 Canadian lineup has four drinks and one baked good, all built around the same Pumpkin Spice syrup. Matthew Feaver, Tim Hortons' Head of Innovation, said the flavour "evokes the warm and cozy vibes of Fall" when he announced the return on September 1.

Pumpkin Spice Latte

The Pumpkin Spice Latte is espresso and steamed milk flavoured with Pumpkin Spice syrup, served hot. It builds on the standard Tim Hortons latte, which comes in small, medium and large, so the Pumpkin Spice version uses the same three sizes.

Pumpkin Spice Iced Latte

The Iced Latte is the same flavour served cold, which Tim Hortons pitches as the lighter, chilled version of the latte. The closest everyday drink is the Tim Hortons iced latte, which is 90 to 140 calories unsweetened before any syrup is added.

Pumpkin Spice Iced Capp

The Pumpkin Spice Iced Capp is the chain's frozen, blended coffee with Pumpkin Spice flavour and whipped topping. It is the richest drink in the lineup, because the Original Iced Capp base already runs 260 to 430 calories before the pumpkin syrup and topping.

Pumpkin Spice Cold Brew

The Cold Brew pairs slow-steeped cold brew coffee with Pumpkin Spice syrup, for a smoother, less milky option. Tim Hortons' regular Original Cold Brew is the closest everyday version.

Pumpkin Spice Muffin

The only food in the lineup is the Pumpkin Spice Muffin, a spiced pumpkin muffin with a sweet cream cheese filling. At 390 calories in the official August 2025 guide, it is one of the heavier items on the muffins menu.

How Many Calories Are in Tim Hortons Pumpkin Spice Drinks?

A Pumpkin Spice drink has the calories of its base drink plus 25 calories for every pump of Pumpkin Spice syrup. Tim Hortons' Canadian nutrition guide lists the syrup separately (25 calories, 5 g sugar and 10 mg sodium per pump) instead of giving totals for each seasonal drink. A Worked Example

Take a medium latte at 140 calories. With three pumps of Pumpkin Spice syrup it would come to 140 + 75 = 215 calories and 13 + 15 = 28 g of sugar, before whipped topping. Tim Hortons does not publish its pump count for each size, so ask at the counter if you are tracking closely. Third-party trackers put the finished hot Pumpkin Spice Latte at about 230 calories for a small and 380 for a large, which fits this method once topping is included.

How Much Does Pumpkin Spice Cost at Tim Hortons?

Pumpkin Spice drinks cost roughly $3.49 to $5.19 before tax in Canada, depending on the drink, the size and the restaurant. Tim Hortons does not publish national prices for seasonal items, and franchise owners set their own, so any single price you see online is one restaurant's price.

How Can You Order Pumpkin Spice Your Way?

You can change almost every Pumpkin Spice drink when you order. The syrup is the part that controls sweetness and most of the added calories.

  1. Choose the base: hot latte, iced latte, Iced Capp or cold brew.
  2. Pick a size: small, medium or large.
  3. Set the syrup: ask for fewer pumps to save about 25 calories and 5 g of sugar each.
  4. Decide on the topping: skip the whipped topping on the Iced Capp if you want it lighter.
  5. Order ahead: use the Tim Hortons app to check your restaurant still has it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tim Hortons Pumpkin Spice back in 2026?

Yes. Tim Hortons brought Pumpkin Spice back in Canada on September 1, 2026, and the lineup is available until November 1, 2026 at participating restaurants.

When does Tim Hortons Pumpkin Spice end?

The 2026 Pumpkin Spice lineup runs until November 1, 2026, according to Tim Hortons. Individual restaurants can sell out of the syrup or muffins earlier, so check the Tim Hortons app near the end of October.

Does Tim Hortons have a Pumpkin Spice Latte?

Yes. The Pumpkin Spice Latte is espresso and steamed milk flavoured with pumpkin spice syrup, and it comes in small, medium and large. An iced version, the Pumpkin Spice Iced Latte, is also on the 2026 menu.

How many calories are in a Tim Hortons Pumpkin Spice Latte?

Tim Hortons does not publish a total for the Pumpkin Spice Latte, but each pump of Pumpkin Spice syrup is 25 calories and 5 g of sugar. A plain latte is 90, 140 or 190 calories by size, and third-party trackers list the finished Pumpkin Spice Latte at about 230 to 380 calories.

How much is a Pumpkin Spice Latte at Tim Hortons?

Expect to pay about $3.79 to $5.19 before tax, depending on size and location. Tim Hortons does not publish a national price, and restaurants set their own.

Does Tim Hortons have a Pumpkin Spice donut or Timbits in Canada?

Not in the 2026 Canadian lineup. Tim Hortons Canada's September 1, 2026 announcement lists four drinks and the Pumpkin Spice Muffin; the Pumpkin Spice Donut is part of the U.S. fall menu.

What is in the Tim Hortons Pumpkin Spice Muffin?

It is a pumpkin spice muffin with a sweet cream cheese filling. The August 2025 Canadian nutrition guide lists it at 390 calories, 58 g of carbohydrates and 31 g of sugar.

Can I get less Pumpkin Spice syrup in my drink?

Yes. Ask for fewer pumps at the counter or adjust it in the Tim Hortons app. Each pump you drop removes about 25 calories and 5 g of sugar.

Is the Tim Hortons Pumpkin Spice Cold Brew sweet?

It is sweetened by the Pumpkin Spice syrup, so it is sweeter than a black cold brew. For comparison, an Original Cold Brew is 120 to 240 calories by size before any Pumpkin Spice syrup.

What else is new on the Tim Hortons fall menu in 2026?

A White Cheddar Hashbrown launched with Pumpkin Spice on September 1, 2026. It is made with 100% Canadian potatoes and seasoned with white cheddar and herbs.

Is Tim Hortons Pumpkin Spice the same in Canada and the U.S.?

No. The U.S. menu launched earlier, on August 12, 2026, and adds a Pumpkin Spice Donut plus Cinnamon Caramel Apple drinks. Canada has the Pumpkin Spice Iced Latte, Cold Brew and Muffin instead.
