Wildberry Hibiscus Lemonade Quencher is the lemonade version of Wildberry Hibiscus in the Tim Hortons Fruit Quenchers lineup. It is listed in 2 sizes: Small $2.19, Medium $2.69, with each size step adding $0.50.

Other ways to order Wildberry Hibiscus

Wildberry Hibiscus is also listed as a Sparkling Quencher (S $2.69 · M $3.19 · L $3.69) and a Frozen Quencher (M $3.89 · L $4.39). At Medium, the only size all three are listed in, the Lemonade version is the lowest-priced of the three.

Common questions

How much is a medium Wildberry Hibiscus Lemonade Quencher?

$2.69 CAD before tax, as last verified. Prices vary by location and province.

Is it priced differently from other Lemonade Quenchers?

Yes. The other five Lemonade Quenchers are listed at S $2.29 · M $2.79 · L $3.29; Wildberry Hibiscus Lemonade Quencher is listed at S $2.19 · M $2.69.

Is the Wildberry Hibiscus Lemonade Quencher caffeine-free and dairy-free?

Independent menu guides consistently list Fruit Quenchers as caffeine-free and dairy-free. If you have an allergy, confirm with Tim Hortons' official allergen information before ordering.

About Fruit Quenchers

In a June 14, 2021 press release, Tim Hortons said its Real Fruit Quenchers “are made from real fruit juice from concentrate.”
