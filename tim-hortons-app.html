What the Tim Hortons App Can Do

Mobile ordering, pickup options, and how the Tims Rewards points program works.

← Back to the full menu

Mobile Ordering

The app lets you order ahead and skip the counter line, choosing pickup in-store, at the drive-thru, or curbside depending on the location. Some locations also support delivery through the app.

Tims Rewards Points

Tims Rewards is the built-in loyalty program: you earn 10 points for every dollar spent, and points go toward free menu items. Points are added automatically on app orders, or when you scan your Tims Rewards card/number on an in-store purchase.

Other Features

Beyond ordering, the app carries personalized offers, contests like Roll Up To WIN and the Tims NHL Hockey Challenge, and Tims Financial — a no-annual-fee credit card managed from inside the app.

Getting the App

The Tim Hortons app is free on the Apple App Store and Google Play. You'll need an account to collect Tims Rewards points and save a payment method or gift card balance — see our gift card balance guide if you're loading a Tims Card into the app.
