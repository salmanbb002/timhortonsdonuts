Every classic and limited-time Tim Hortons donut, with current listed prices and calories in one place.

Full Donut Menu

How Much Are Tim Hortons Donuts?

A single Tim Hortons donut runs from about $2.09 to $2.39 CAD depending on the variety, with filled and specialty donuts like the Boston Cream or Spiced Vanilla Filled Ring priced slightly higher than classic glazed and dipped donuts. Assorted boxes are the better value per donut: a 6-pack runs about $9.39 and a 12-pack about $17.39, before tax.

Popular Donut Types

Tim Hortons' donut case is built around a few families: classic yeast donuts (Honey Dip, Chocolate Dip, Old Fashioned Plain), cake donuts (Sour Cream Glazed, Chocolate Glazed, Toasted Coconut), cream-filled donuts (Boston Cream, Biscoff Boston Cream), and seasonal or limited-time releases like the Canada Celebration Donut and the Spiced Vanilla Filled Ring Donut. Assorted 6 and 12 packs pull from whatever is in the bakery case that day.

FAQ

What is the cheapest Tim Hortons donut?

Classic donuts such as the Honey Dip, Chocolate Dip, Old Fashioned Plain and Sour Cream Glazed are the lowest-priced individual donuts, listed around $2.09 CAD.

How many calories are in a Tim Hortons donut?

Individual donuts range from about 220 calories (Chocolate Dip) to 360 calories (Toasted Coconut). Assorted boxes list a total for the whole box, not a per-donut figure.

Are Tim Hortons donuts always available?

Limited-time and seasonal donuts, like the Biscoff Boston Cream or Spiced Vanilla Filled Ring, depend on what a location is currently carrying. Assorted boxes depend on what's left in the bakery case, so a requested flavour may be substituted.
