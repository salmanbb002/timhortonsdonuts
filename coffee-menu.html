What Coffee Does Tim Hortons Use?

Tim Hortons brews its Original Blend, a medium roast made from 100% Arabica beans, in every restaurant. Two other brewed coffees sit next to it: Dark Roast and Decaf. The Original Blend and Dark Roast are priced the same, from $1.59 for a small.

The same Original Blend is sold in grocery stores in Canada and the U.S. as ground coffee, whole beans and K-Cup pods. The retail packaging says the beans come from several growing regions and are blended and roasted in Canada. If you want the restaurant taste at home, that is the bag to buy. Our guide to Tim Hortons coffee beans compares bag sizes, prices and where to buy them.

What Sizes Does Tim Hortons Coffee Come In?

Tim Hortons brewed coffee comes in four sizes in Canada: small (10 oz), medium (14 oz), large (20 oz) and extra large (24 oz). The chain resized its cups in 2012, so today's small is the old medium and today's large is the old extra large. Espresso drinks and iced drinks stop at large.

How Much Caffeine Is in Tim Hortons Coffee?

A medium Tim Hortons brewed coffee has about 205 mg of caffeine, and an extra large has about 330 mg. These figures come from caffeine data Tim Hortons' research and development team published in 2008. The chain does not print caffeine on its current Canadian menu, so treat them as close estimates.

Health Canada advises healthy adults to keep caffeine to 400 mg a day. One extra large brewed coffee uses more than 80% of that, and a large plus a small already goes over it (410 mg).

What Is a Double-Double at Tim Hortons?

A double-double is a coffee with two creams and two sugars. The Dictionary of Canadianisms on Historical Principles traces the term to 1985, and the Canadian Oxford Dictionary marks it as Canadian English. It is still tied closely to Tim Hortons.

"Two" scales with the cup. Tim Hortons' August 2025 nutrition guide puts a medium double-double at 200 calories. The guide's own add-ins explain the total: a medium black coffee (4 calories), plus two medium creams (2 × 60) and two medium sugars (2 × 35), comes to 194 calories. "Regular", "triple-triple" and "deux-deux" are explained in our guide to ordering at Tim Hortons.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a coffee at Tim Hortons?

A Tim Hortons brewed coffee costs $1.59 for a small, $1.83 for a medium, $2.07 for a large and $2.35 for an extra large. These are listed reference prices in CAD before tax, and they vary by province and restaurant.

What coffee beans does Tim Hortons use?

Tim Hortons uses 100% Arabica beans for its Original Blend. The retail packaging says the beans come from several growing regions and are blended and roasted in Canada.

What is a double-double?

A double-double is coffee with two creams and two sugars. A medium double-double is 200 calories, according to Tim Hortons' August 2025 nutrition guide.

How much caffeine is in a medium Tim Hortons coffee?

A medium (14 oz) Tim Hortons brewed coffee has about 205 mg of caffeine. That estimate comes from caffeine data Tim Hortons published in 2008; current menus do not list caffeine.

What sizes does Tim Hortons coffee come in?

Brewed coffee comes in small (10 oz), medium (14 oz), large (20 oz) and extra large (24 oz). Lattes, cappuccinos and iced drinks come in small, medium and large.

Is Tim Hortons Dark Roast stronger than the Original Blend?

Dark Roast tastes bolder, but it costs the same and has the same calories as the Original Blend. Tim Hortons does not publish a caffeine difference between the two in Canada.

How many calories are in a Tim Hortons black coffee?

A black Tim Hortons brewed coffee has 3 to 5 calories, from small to extra large. Almost all the calories in a coffee come from cream, milk, sugar or syrup.

How many calories are in a medium Iced Capp?

A medium Original Iced Capp has 330 calories and 42 g of sugar. Flavoured versions are higher; a medium Vanilla Iced Capp has 540 calories.

Can you buy Tim Hortons coffee in grocery stores?

Yes. Tim Hortons Original Blend, Dark Roast and Decaf are sold in grocery stores as ground coffee, whole beans and K-Cup pods.

Is Tim Hortons coffee available all day?

Yes. Brewed coffee and espresso drinks are served for as long as a restaurant is open, including at 24-hour drive-thru locations.
